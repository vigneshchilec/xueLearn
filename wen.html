How Wen works

Three simple ways Wen becomes part of your child's day

No lesson plans. No drills. Just a friend who happens to speak two languages.

💬
STEP ONE
Wen talks with your child
Real, gentle conversation in Chinese and English — about their day, their toys, their questions. Not a script.
📖
STEP TWO
Wen tells stories together
Interactive, bilingual tales your child helps shape by choosing what happens next — one tap, one character at a time.
🌱
STEP THREE
Wen remembers and grows with them
Wen notices what your child loves and gently brings more of it — new words, new stories, new little inside jokes.
